## [0.1.0] — 2026-10-02

### Added

- The night: relight the dead lights falling out of the sky before your own
  lantern goes out, and keep it lit until sunrise.
- A stick-figure wizard drawn entirely in code — two-bone IK, a lantern on a
  damped pendulum, posture driven by how much light is left.
- The sky as the clock: dusk, deep night, the turn, first light, sunrise.
- Five lights that never went out — ember, beacon, hush, bloom, ward — each
  with its own mark, sound and visible effect.
- Sound synthesised at runtime, no audio file; the mix measured with an
  analyser rather than guessed, with a limiter on the output.
- Motes and screen shake; a contrast halo that keeps the figure readable on
  any sky.
- A scroll that serves as title, rules, pause and result, in English and
  Burmese; the rules page shows the game's own pieces instead of describing
  them.
- Pause on `P`, `Esc` or losing focus.
